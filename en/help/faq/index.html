How do your clothes fit?
Fits vary by piece: coats and knitwear are often relaxed or oversized, jeans follow your usual waist size. Every product page lists the fit, and the size guide has exact measurements in cm. Size guide.
Will sold-out items come back?
Sometimes. Core pieces such as jeans and basics are restocked; seasonal pieces are usually made once. Use the newsletter to hear about restocks and new collections first.
How long does delivery take?
2–5 working days anywhere in the EU, usually 1–3 in the Baltics. Orders before 14:00 Vilnius time ship the same working day. Shipping details.
How much is shipping?
€4.99 flat to any EU country. Free on orders of €60 or more.
Do you ship outside the EU?
Not yet.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal and Klarna. Payments are processed by Stripe.
Can I return something?
Yes, within 14 days of delivery, no reason needed. Returns policy.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Contact us as soon as possible. If it has not shipped yet, we can change or cancel it. After that, use a return.
How do I use a discount code?
Enter it in the discount field at checkout. One code per order. Codes cannot be applied after the order is placed.
How do I care for wool, knitwear and denim?
Knitwear: wash cold on a wool cycle and dry flat. Wool coats: dry clean or steam. Denim: wash inside out at 30°C, no tumble dryer. Care instructions are on every product page.
